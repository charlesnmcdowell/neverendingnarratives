Adventurer

Create your character, learn skills and put together a party worth trusting. Take jobs, join factions, build relationships and raise a family. When an adventure goes badly, your next life can carry the story on.

  • Turn-based tactical combat and party building
  • Factions, relationships and a complete story campaign
  • For players who enjoy a slower, deeper RPG

Adventurer: Expeditions

Meet Hiro, a samurai with purple locs and very little patience for an ambush. Follow the story through voiced scenes and animated battles. He attacks automatically; you choose when to counter, power up or land a finisher.

  • Play the opening chapter now
  • Tap or click skills during cinematic sword fights

About

Made by Hiro Protagonist

Neverending Narratives is my home for browser RPGs, fantasy novels and LitRPG audiobooks. Adventurer is the complete game. Expeditions is my current project, with a playable opening chapter and more story on the way.
